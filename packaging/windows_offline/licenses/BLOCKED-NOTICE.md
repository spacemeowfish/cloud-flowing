# Assets whose redistribution is not cleared

This notice is an engineering distribution gate, not legal advice. Presence of
an asset in a local validation directory does not mean that the asset may be
shared with colleagues or uploaded to a public release.

The following rights remain unresolved:

- **Qwen2.5-3B GGUF:** the Qwen Research License grants rights for
  non-commercial research or evaluation only and says commercial users must
  request a separate license. No written authorization for this company's
  commercial internal-test redistribution is on file.
- **LFM2.5-1.2B GGUF:** LFM Open License 1.0 conditions commercial rights on
  the user or Legal Entity not meeting its USD 10 million annual-revenue
  threshold. The company has not provided a written eligibility determination.
- **ZipVoice model directory:** the exact sherpa-onnx distribution contains
  eSpeak NG data, but the package audit has not established the exact eSpeak
  version, corresponding COPYING/NOTICE material, or source-code compliance.
  The applicable Emilia training-data restrictions also have not been closed.
- **`vocos_24khz.onnx`:** upstream Vocos is MIT, but no license or provenance
  notice accompanying the exact exported ONNX file has been located.
- **`news-female1`, `male1`, `female1`, and `female2` WAV files:** there is no
  written evidence covering the recordings, speaker or voice identity rights,
  and redistribution permission.

Until each relevant item is resolved in writing, a complete local validation
bundle containing it must remain marked `NON_DISTRIBUTABLE_LOCAL_VALIDATION`,
must not be given to colleagues, and must not be uploaded to GitHub Releases.
